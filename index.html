The problem

Applying to jobs is a second job — and the slow applicants lose.

Every application is the same hour of work: find the posting, re-read the JD, tweak the resume, retype your details into yet another portal, answer the same screening questions, log it somewhere. Multiply by thirty roles and the hunt eats the time you should spend preparing for interviews and building skills.

Worse, timing is everything. Postings collect hundreds of applications in their first days; by the time a role reaches your weekend shortlist, the first batch is already being screened. The candidates who apply early with a tailored resume win — not the ones who apply most.

The existing "solutions" are worse than the problem: auto-apply bots that blast one generic resume at hundreds of jobs, invent experience, and get accounts flagged. Recruiters can smell them. JobSweep is the opposite approach — automate the grind, keep the judgement, never fake a line.
